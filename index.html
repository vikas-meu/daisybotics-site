The flower on the camera and the box is the Daisy Botics mark. Robots can look friendly, and so can learning to build them.

> whoami

Why daisies, on a robotics site?

Because robots shouldn't feel cold. Daisy Botics started with one idea: build affordable robots, and show people that they can build them too.

I'm Vikas Singh Thakur, an Electronics & Telecommunication student at SSIPMT Raipur. I like taking robots from a sketch on paper to actually working in front of judges, whether that's an autonomous field robot, a teleoperated humanoid, or a swarm of vision-guided cars.

The skeleton with flowers is a reminder I keep on my desk. Under every machine there's a frame of bones and wires. What grows on it is up to the builder.
